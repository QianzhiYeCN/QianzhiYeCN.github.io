I am a Ph.D. student with the College of Information Science and Technology at Jinan University in Guangzhou, China. I received my M.S. degree from the School of Computer and Information Engineering at Jiangxi Agricultural University in 2023. My research interests include multi-label learning, partial-label learning, semi-supervised learning, and feature selection. During my Ph.D. studies, I have also begun exploring brain-computer interfaces, with a focus on fMRI-based semantic decoding.

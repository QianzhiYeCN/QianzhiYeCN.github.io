Qianzhi Ye 叶倩芝

Ph.D. Student
Jinan University

  • ●Guangzhou, China

I am a Ph.D. student with the College of Information Science and Technology at Jinan University in Guangzhou, China. I received my M.S. degree from the School of Computer and Information Engineering at Jiangxi Agricultural University in 2023. My research focuses on multi-label learning, partial-label learning, semi-supervised learning, label enhancement, and feature selection.
